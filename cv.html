Executive Profile

I build and lead teams that turn complex problems into systems people can understand, trust, and use. My background in anthropology and engineering shapes how I lead: understand the people and the mission, make the problem clear, and build the organization capable of solving it. I connect technical depth with business judgment to help institutions serve people better.
Founder and CEO of Bast AI, IBM Distinguished Engineer (appointed 2016), and board trustee. My work brings together the realities of running a business and the realities of how people learn, make decisions, and care for one another.

The work in five outcomes

  • Led IBM's workforce transformation, upskilling and reskilling 23,000 people in 16 months.
  • Created the data-science profession through which more than 10,000 data scientists have been certified globally.
  • Owned and grew two P&Ls by double digits, helping establish the foundation for AI at IBM.
  • Created technology spanning 27 issued IBM patents and five Bast patents.
  • Built Bast over four years, securing $1.5M in revenue and $750K in investment.

Professional Experience

Founder and CEO, Bast AI · October 2022–present
Founded Bast to build explainable AI for healthcare and other regulated institutions. Lead engineers and domain experts, giving each the authority to do their best work and setting the standards that connect them. Own client outcomes from scoping through production.
We build the knowledge layer underneath AI, structuring domain expertise so answers can be traced to evidence and unresolved terms are flagged. From BastCare to institutional deployments, our work helps people understand care and access expert knowledge in difficult environments.
Led Bast’s transition to agentic delivery, with human review of every change. Build the teams, partnerships, and evaluation practices needed to deliver dependable systems and sustain the business.
Bast architecture provides an AI system that uses 70–85% fewer tokens per interaction versus standard retrieval-augmented generation.
IBM business and technical leadership · 23 years
Owned and grew the Content Intelligence and AI at Scale businesses, carrying responsibility for their P&Ls and leading teams to deliver business results. Established the Trustworthy AI Center of Excellence and recruited its leadership.
As Chief Data Officer for IBM’s services division, led workforce transformation through upskilling and reskilling. Used the urgency of enterprise change to help people move into new kinds of work, and directed large-scale endpoint remediation to address security and data-protection requirements.
Created and secured The Open Group accreditation for the data-science profession, building the skills and professional standards needed to deliver AI at global scale.
