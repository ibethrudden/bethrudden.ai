Privacy & accessibility

A site you can
understand.

This is a static professional site. It does not sell visitor data or run advertising pixels. Optional analytics load only when a visitor chooses to allow them.

Analytics

Optional Google Analytics 4 helps us understand which pages, CV downloads, outbound links, and email links are useful. Analytics are off until you choose “Allow analytics” in the cookie notice. Choosing “Necessary only” keeps Google Analytics from loading. The measurement ID is G-FL8JCB0PXZ.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores a preference named br_cookie_consent to remember your choice. When analytics are allowed, Google Analytics may set analytics cookies according to Google’s controls and retention settings.

Contact links

Email links open your email application. This site has no inquiry form and does not store messages. You decide whether to send an email.

Accessibility

The site uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text, and layouts that adapt to screen size. If something gets in your way, please let me know.

Questions

For privacy or accessibility questions, email beth@bast.ai.
